The 10 Better… Rules

Reminders, not demands. Choose the rule that helps you today.

  1. 1Better, not perfect.
    Progress matters more than perfection.
  2. 2Start small.
    Make the first step easy enough to begin.
  3. 3Make it easy.
    Reduce friction instead of relying on willpower.
  4. 4Be consistent.
    Repeat useful actions when you can.
  5. 5Do not depend on motivation.
    Use a simple plan for ordinary days.
  6. 6Small improvement counts.
    A little progress is still progress.
  7. 7Learn from what happens.
    Every attempt gives you information.
  8. 8Restart after a missed day.
    A pause is not failure; begin again.
  9. 9Focus on your own progress.
    Compare yourself with your earlier self.
  10. 10Keep going sustainably.
    Rest when needed and choose a maintainable pace.
The short version: LEARN → DO → REPEAT
Learn one useful idea. Take one small action. Repeat, reflect, and adjust.

How the counters work
